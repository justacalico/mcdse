MCDSE

A save-file editor for Minecraft Dungeons — rebuilt in Flutter for desktop.

Linux · Windows · macOS

Features

  • Open and save both encrypted (.dat) and decrypted (.json) character files — format converts automatically
  • Edit currencies, character level, power, and enchantment points
  • Full inventory & storage chest management — filter, add, duplicate, delete, and move items
  • Per-item editing: power, rarity, flags, enchantments, gilded enchantments, armor properties
  • Reads item art and localized names from your game's own pak files, with a built-in fallback mode
  • Progress counters & mob kills, portal unlock, recent files, drag-and-drop
